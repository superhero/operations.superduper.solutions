# Code of Conduct

This is a condensed, project-specific adaptation of [Contributor Covenant 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/). Refer to the original for context and clarification where this version is unclear.

## Commitment

The project maintainer is committed to a welcoming, safe, and equitable environment for everyone participating in good faith.

All participants, including the maintainer, must follow this Code and treat others with respect, regardless of background or personal differences.

## Expected Conduct

- **Stay relevant and helpful.** Respect the project's purpose and support others' participation.
- **Communicate respectfully.** Be honest and direct, allowing for differences in background, language, and communication style.
- **Disagree constructively.** Consider arguments on their merits. Give and accept feedback about ideas, code, and decisions—not people.
- **Take responsibility.** Acknowledge mistakes, correct them, and address problems caused by your conduct.

Good-faith technical criticism, disagreement, or rejection of a contribution is not, by itself, misconduct.

## Prohibited Conduct

- **Harassment and personal attacks.** Ignoring explicit personal boundaries, continuing unwanted attention after a request to stop, or insulting or demeaning people.
- **Stereotyping or discrimination.** Judging people's character or abilities, or treating them unfairly, based on identity or personal characteristics.
- **Inappropriate sexual conduct.** Sexual attention, content, or intimate behavior inappropriate to the project's context.
- **Privacy violations.** Sharing or misusing personal or confidential information without permission.
- **Harm and disruption.** Causing or threatening harm, or deliberately obstructing others' participation.
- **Other misuse.** Impersonation, evading enforcement, failing to credit sources, spam, or unrelated promotion.

Threatening or encouraging these behaviors is also prohibited. Content depicting or describing misconduct must be presented responsibly and with appropriate context; relevant discussion or evidence is not itself a violation.

## Reporting an Issue

Report possible violations privately to the project maintainer at **[erik.landvall@superduper.solutions](mailto:erik.landvall@superduper.solutions)**. Describe what happened, when and where, and include relevant links or screenshots. Do not report through public issues, pull requests, or discussions.

The maintainer will review each report and the available evidence, seek clarification where needed, and communicate decisions and restrictions privately wherever practical. Information will be shared only as needed to investigate and respond.

## Enforcement and Resolution

The maintainer will assess each report individually and apply proportionate measures based on the evidence, severity, impact, and any previous violations. These measures need not follow a fixed sequence:

1. **Warning.** A private written notice explaining the violation and required changes when clarification and correction are sufficient.
2. **Limited participation.** Restrictions on specific activities, channels, or interactions for repeated or more serious violations, with a stated duration.
3. **Temporary suspension.** Exclusion for serious or persistent violations, with a stated duration and any conditions for return.
4. **Permanent ban.** Permanent exclusion from maintainer-controlled project spaces for severe or unresolved repeated violations.

Corrective action may include correcting or removing content, acknowledging responsibility, or offering a private apology. The maintainer may also remove, edit, or reject content that violates this Code.

Respect all restrictions and conditions for return. Do not evade them through another account or person. Corrective action does not automatically restore participation.

## Scope

This Code applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and communication channels. It also applies when officially representing the project through project accounts, email addresses, or appointed roles at events.
