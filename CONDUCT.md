# Code of Conduct

## Commitment

The project maintainer is committed to making participation in this project welcoming, safe, and equitable.

Everyone participating in good faith and in accordance with this Code of Conduct is welcome. Participants are expected to treat one another with respect and consideration, regardless of background or personal differences.

These standards apply to everyone participating in the project, including the project maintainer.

## Encouraged Behaviors

Participants may differ in social norms, culture, background, and native language. Words and actions can therefore be interpreted differently from how they were intended.

Positive participation includes:

1. **Respecting the project's purpose.** Keep contributions and discussions relevant to the project and the space in which they occur.
2. **Communicating kindly and honestly.** Be clear and direct without making disagreements personal.
3. **Respecting different viewpoints and experiences.** Consider arguments on their merits and allow room for good-faith disagreement.
4. **Taking responsibility.** Stand behind your contributions, acknowledge mistakes, and correct them where possible.
5. **Giving and accepting constructive feedback.** Focus criticism on ideas, code, and decisions rather than the person presenting them.
6. **Addressing harm.** Make reasonable efforts to resolve problems caused by your conduct.
7. **Supporting productive collaboration.** Behave in ways that help others participate and contribute.

## Restricted Behaviors

The following behaviors, including threats or encouragement of them, violate this Code of Conduct:

1. **Harassment.** Violating explicitly expressed personal boundaries or continuing unwanted personal attention after a clear request to stop.
2. **Character attacks.** Making insulting, demeaning, or pejorative comments directed at a participant or group of people.
3. **Stereotyping or discrimination.** Judging someone's character or abilities, or treating them unfairly, based on their identity or personal characteristics.
4. **Inappropriate sexual conduct.** Introducing sexual attention, comments, imagery, or other intimate behavior that is inappropriate to the project's context.
5. **Violating privacy or confidentiality.** Sharing or misusing someone's personal or confidential information without permission.
6. **Endangerment.** Causing, encouraging, or threatening violence or other harm toward a person or group.
7. **Other harmful conduct.** Behaving in ways that threaten participants' safety or deliberately disrupt their ability to participate in the project.

### Other Restrictions

1. **Misleading identity.** Impersonating another person or misrepresenting your identity to evade enforcement actions.
2. **Failing to credit sources.** Contributing content without appropriate attribution to its sources.
3. **Unsolicited promotion.** Posting spam, advertising, or other promotional content unrelated to the project's purpose.
4. **Irresponsible communication.** Sharing content that depicts or describes restricted behavior without appropriate context, or using it to encourage that behavior. Relevant discussion or evidence of misconduct is not itself a violation.

## Reporting an Issue

Not every disagreement or conflict is a Code of Conduct violation. Good-faith technical criticism, disagreement with a decision, or rejection of a contribution does not, by itself, constitute misconduct.

To report a possible violation, **write an email to the project maintainer: [erik.landvall@superduper.solutions](mailto:erik.landvall@superduper.solutions).** Include a description of the incident, when and where it occurred, and any relevant links or screenshots.

Do not submit reports through public issues, pull requests, or discussions. Reports will be reviewed discreetly, and information will be shared only as needed to investigate and respond.

The project maintainer will review each report, consider the available evidence, and seek clarification where necessary. Relevant decisions and any resulting restrictions will be communicated privately to the people involved wherever practical.

## Enforcement and Resolution

The project maintainer will assess each report individually and apply measures proportionate to the severity and impact of the conduct, taking previous violations into account.

The measures below are guidelines, not mandatory sequential steps. Serious or repeated violations may result in immediate suspension or permanent removal.

### 1. Warning

**When:** A violation that can reasonably be addressed by clarifying expectations and requiring the conduct to stop.

**Consequence:** A private, written warning from the project maintainer identifying the conduct and explaining the expected change.

**Resolution:** Stop the conduct and address its effects where possible. Appropriate steps may include correcting a statement, removing content, acknowledging responsibility, or offering a private apology.

### 2. Temporarily Limited Activities

**When:** A repeated violation following a warning, or a more serious incident that warrants restrictions on particular activities.

**Consequence:** A private, written notice specifying the restrictions and their duration. Restrictions may apply to particular discussions, communication channels, contribution activities, or contact with specific participants.

**Resolution:** Respect the restrictions, make any specified corrections, and resume the affected activities only when the restrictions have ended.

### 3. Temporary Suspension

**When:** A pattern of violations that previous measures have not resolved, or a single serious violation.

**Consequence:** Temporary exclusion from project participation. The project maintainer will communicate the duration and any conditions for returning.

**Resolution:** Respect the suspension, do not attempt to evade it through another account or person, and meet the stated conditions before returning.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**When:** Repeated violations that previous measures have not resolved, or conduct sufficiently serious to warrant ending participation immediately.

**Consequence:** Permanent exclusion from participation in project spaces controlled by the project maintainer.

**Resolution:** Do not attempt to circumvent the ban. Acknowledging or correcting the conduct does not automatically restore participation privileges.

The project maintainer may also remove, edit, or reject content that violates this Code of Conduct. Enforcement decisions will be based on the conduct and available evidence, with the aim of protecting participants and maintaining productive collaboration.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces, including repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and project communication channels.

It also applies when someone officially represents the project in public or other spaces. Examples include using an official project email address, posting through an official project social media account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event.
